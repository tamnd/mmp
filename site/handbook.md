Volumes 16 and 17 are dated reports. They are rewritten at each milestone.

## Two states

Each theorem section shows two states. The Lean part shows the Lean status of the node. The Notes and history part shows the state in the literature: theorem, claimed, conjecture or refuted. A Lean proof of a statement that is only claimed in the literature does not change the literature state.

## Who writes and who checks

AI models draft much of the text. Each section names its drafter and its reviewers. A section is reviewed when a person who knows the subject signs it.

## The state

The handbook is in [tamnd/mmp-handbook](https://github.com/tamnd/mmp-handbook). The project is at milestone S0, and no volume is released yet. Volume 0 and P0 are planned as drafts at milestone S1, and Volume 0 is planned for release at milestone S2.

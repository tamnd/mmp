## Before you start

The handbook has two parts. The volumes P0 to P8 teach the algebraic geometry that the MMP uses. They start at classical varieties and end at rational curves, resolution, toric varieties and the first discrepancies. The volumes 0 to 17 teach the program itself. They start at divisors and cones and end at the open problems.

There are three kinds of readers:

- a student with a first course in algebra and topology starts at P0;
- a student who knows Hartshorne II, III and V, or Vakil, takes the fast path and starts at Volume 0;
- a mathematician who does not read Lean and wants the status of a statement and its proof reads Volume 0 and then the volume of the statement.

Each theorem section has six parts in a fixed order: Statement, Proof in words, Examples, Exercises, Lean, and Notes and history. The Lean part shows the Lean status of the node. The Notes and history part shows the state in the literature. The two states are independent.

## The paths

A path is a list of chapters, not of volumes.

| Path | For | Chapters | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full | a strong undergraduate | P0 to P8 in order, with P6 in parallel with P5 or P7; then Volumes 0 to 8 | four to five semesters for P, two more for 0 to 8 |
| Fast | a student who knows Hartshorne II, III and V, or Vakil | selected chapters of P0 to P8, with all of P7; then Volumes 0 to 8 | two semesters for P |
| Surfaces first | a student who wants Volume 2 only | P0; P1 Ch. 1 to 9; P3; P4 Ch. 1 to 7; P5; P8 Ch. 4, 9 to 11; then Volumes 0 and 2 | three semesters |
| Toric first | a student from combinatorics or convex geometry, or a Lean contributor of route R3 | P0 Ch. 1 to 4; P8 Part C; Volume 3 with its Lean parts; then P3 Ch. 8 to 11 and Volume 4 Ch. 1 to 3 | one semester |
| Complex-analytic | a student who knows Huybrechts | skip P6; P1, P3 and P4 quickly; then the fast path from P7 | two semesters |
| Arithmetic | a student who knows Liu or Silverman | P5 Ch. 5 to 12; P7; P8; then Volumes 0 to 8, and Volume 11 early | two semesters |
| BCHM seminar | a student who knows KM98 Ch. 1 to 3 | Volume 4 Ch. 1 to 4; Volume 5; Volume 7; Volume 8 Ch. 1 and 2; Volume 9 section by section | one semester |
| Boundedness | a student after the BCHM path | Volume 10 Ch. 1 to 3; Volume 12; Volume 16 Ch. 1 | one semester |
| Shape first | a reader who wants the program before the proofs | Volume 0; the first chapter of each of Volumes 1 to 12; Volume 17 | four weeks |
| Lean contributor | a person who wants to write Lean for the project | Volume 0 Ch. 4; P3 Ch. 8 and 9 with their Lean parts; Volume 4 Ch. 1; the Lean parts of Volume 3; spec 06 | four weeks |

## The learner kit

Each release of the handbook comes with a status table by dimension, a table of the black boxes with their Lean hypotheses, a glossary, a list of running examples, and notebooks for the exercises marked (M). No exercise of the handbook needs Lean. Volume 0 Ch. 4 teaches enough Lean to read a statement of the program.

## How to read the routes

Nobody can prove all of the open minimal model program on a schedule. Termination in dimension 5 and abundance in dimension 4 have resisted experts for decades. Most open targets of the routes have a difficulty of 4 or 5 on a scale from 1 to 5. A difficulty of 5 means that experts have no plausible strategy. The project does not promise a date for any open target.

The year 2026 changes one thing. For some central targets a candidate proof already exists: a preprint that no referee has read, an AI manuscript, or a Lean proof that no person has read. For those targets the work of the project is to check, not to discover. Routes R1 and R2 are this work.

The weights set the share of the research budget of the agent for the first year. The work on the library for the levels G1 to G3 is outside this budget. The weights are higher where a route has a cheap first node that also serves G2 or G3 (R2, R3), or where the check is cheap and the information value is high (R1).

The weights change only at a milestone report, from the nodes that are proved, refuted or stalled. A route with no progress for two milestones loses half its weight to the route with the best cost per proved node. The milestone report says so in plain words. The project reports a negative result with the same care as a positive one.

## The computation lab

The lab is a method, not a route. It serves all routes and has a weight of 0.05. Much of the MMP is convex geometry over $\mathbb{Z}^n$: toric varieties, toric mlds and toric flips. There a computer can give a complete proof of a finite statement, but only through a certificate that Lean checks. Data from the lab is not a proof. The [lab page](/lab/) lists the runs.

## Target T

Spec 04 names one open target for the level G5, Target T. It is the guess of the project, and no expert has read it yet. Target T is the conjecture 1.9(2') of Han-Liu for the enc 4-fold germs that are toric (node S52). It is open. It is convex geometry over $\mathbb{Z}^4$, on the library of route R3. It is on the reduction graph: the full conjecture in dimension 4 gives termination of lc flips in dimension 4. A toric case gives a human proof of a piece of T(4) that does not depend on AI code. The lab can search the cones first (experiment E2) before a proof. The backups are the questions of Ajit (route R6) and a question on foliated surfaces (route R8). The milestone report of S3 reviews the choice.

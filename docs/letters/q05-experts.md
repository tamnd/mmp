# Letter to expert reviewers (Q-05, Q-19, Q-21)

Subject: a request to read the Lean statements of the minimal model program

Dear colleague,

We are the maintainers of mmp, a public project that states the minimal model program in Lean 4 on Mathlib. The project is at https://github.com/tamnd/mmp and the site is at https://mmp.tamnd.com.

Much of the code and the text of the project is written by models. Lean checks the proofs, and people check the statements and the text. A statement is useful only if an expert agrees that it says what the theorem or the conjecture says. So we ask for your help with the statements of your area. Each statement has a page on the site with the Lean text, the informal statement and the source.

We send this letter to three groups: the MMP and termination group, the boundedness and K-stability group, and the Lean group of Mathlib algebraic geometry. We ask each reader for one hour or more, at the time that suits you.

Our questions are these:

1. Are the statements of the termination, abundance and minimal model families the ones that you would accept? In particular:
   1. "every program terminates" against "some program terminates" in the termination statements;
   2. the fields of a flip: small, ample over the base, and an isomorphism in codimension one;
   3. the numerical dimension in the abundance statements (we use the dimension $\kappa_\sigma$ of Nakayama);
   4. the class of pairs and of coefficients in each statement.
2. We define the log discrepancy over all divisorial valuations of the function field, and not on one log resolution. Is this the right base, also in characteristic $p$?
3. What is the widest published form of the termination of toric flips?
4. Our first research target is the toric case of Han-Liu Conjecture 1.9 (2'). Is it already known? Does it follow at once from the toric ACC of Borisov or from the toric LSC of Ambro?
5. Which routes of our plan have a chance of a new result in two years?
6. Of the definitions of AxiomMath/MMP, which would you read first in an audit? Which choices would change the meaning of the 4-fold termination theorem?
7. Which published results in your area have known gaps, or are not accepted?
8. Which of the 2026 claims would you like to see checked first?
9. For the handbook: which order would you use to teach the minimal model program in one year, and what must a student know before the first volume?

We also look for readers of the handbook. A graduate student can review a section, and a teacher of the standard course can review a prerequisite volume. Some exercises of the handbook come from other books, with a credit line. If you wrote one of these books, we ask for your permission to adapt its exercises. Until we have it, we write new exercises.

You can answer by email, or in an issue of the repository. We will name you as a reviewer only if you agree.

With best regards,

The maintainers of mmp

# 16. Open questions

These are the decisions that the spec does not make. Each one has the options, a recommendation, the milestone by which it must be decided, and who decides.
- "Owner" means the owner of the repositories.
- "Experts" means the outside reviewers of 06.3 (F6) and 14.1 (Q7).
- "Axiom" means the authors of AxiomMath/MMP and AxiomMath/Stacks.

A decision goes into the CHANGELOG of `tamnd/mmp` with its date, and the row here gets the word **decided** and a link. A recommendation is not a decision until the owner signs it.

## 16.1 Questions for the experts

These are mathematical questions that the project cannot decide alone. They go in the first letters (Q-05).

1. Are the statements of 06.2 the ones you would accept? In particular:
   - "every program terminates" against "some program terminates" in S20 to S25 (06.5, risk 1);
   - the fields of `Flip` (small, $K^+$-ample over the base, an isomorphism in codimension one; risk 2);
   - the numerical dimension in the abundance statements (Nakayama's $\kappa_\sigma$; risk 4);
   - the class of pairs and of coefficients in each statement (06.2.3).
2. Which definition of the discrepancy and of the pair classes would you fix (Q-04)? Is "log discrepancy over all divisorial valuations" the right base for char $p$?
3. Is the scope of toric termination in 03.13 right (Q-12)? Which source proves the widest form?
4. Is Target T (04.12) a good first research target? Is the toric case of Han-Liu Conj. 1.9(2') already known, or does it follow at once from Borisov's toric ACC or Ambro's LSC?
5. Which of the routes of 04 has a chance of a new result in two years, and is the key obstruction in 04.1 stated correctly?
6. Of the definitions of AxiomMath/MMP, which would you read first in the audit (A5)? Which choices would change the meaning of the 4-fold termination theorem?
7. Which published results in your area have known gaps, or are not accepted? This sets `expert_status` in the paper records (07.14).
8. Which of the fresh claims of 01.3 would you like to see checked, and which part is self-contained enough to check first (Q-15)?
9. For the handbook: which order would you use to teach Volumes 1, 2, 4 and 5 in one year, and what must a student know from P7 and P8 before Volume 1 (Q-20)?
